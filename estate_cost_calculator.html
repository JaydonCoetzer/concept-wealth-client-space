Assumptions & sources
SARS / Master's figures used (2026, confirm annually):
Estate duty: 20% on the dutiable estate up to R30m, 25% above. Section 4A abatement R3.5m (portable between spouses, up to R7m). Section 4(q) spousal bequests deductible.
Executor fee: 3.5% of the gross value of estate assets, plus 15% VAT.
Master's fee: sliding scale, nil under R250 000, R600 to R400 000, then R200 per R100 000, capped at R7 000.
CGT on death: deemed disposal, R300 000 annual exclusion in the year of death, 40% inclusion, assumed 45% marginal rate (18% effective). Assets to a spouse roll over.
Conveyancing is a rough transfer estimate you can edit, confirm against the Law Society guideline.

This is an illustration, not financial, tax or legal advice. Figures are estimates; a formal estate plan should be prepared with your adviser.
